Gear5ve Watch Face – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Gear5ve is a watch face for Wear OS. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • The watch face contains no code and requests no permissions.
  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • Complications you choose to display (for example battery or steps) are provided by your watch or by other apps you select. Their data is shown on your watch only and is never sent anywhere by Gear5ve.

If you have questions, contact the developer at the email address listed on the Google Play store page.
